Tech is the equipment on your counter

A coffee bar and a clothes shop need different things within reach. We start with how your staff move.

Registers
Countertop screens, tablets or handhelds, set up with your own menu or product list.
Card readers
Fixed at the till, carried to the table or taken out on the van.
Printers
Receipts at the counter, tickets in the kitchen and labels at the scale.
Network
Signal at the counter, and a way to keep selling when it drops.

Pay is how the money arrives

Customers pay with whatever they have in their hand. Your books should show one total either way.

At the counter
Contactless, chip, phones, watches and cash.
Away from it
Payment links, online orders, invoices and deposits.
After the sale
Refunds, tips, split bills and the end-of-day count.

Hub is the people you call

You should not have to work out whether it is the reader, the register or the internet before asking for help.

Installation
We bring the equipment, connect it and run a real sale before we go.
Training
Each person on your team rings up, refunds and closes a day.
Changes
New prices, a new dish, another register or a second location.
Support
One team that has your setup on file.

What we check before we quote

These four answers decide what can stay and what has to change.

Your statement
What you pay for each $100 sold, once every fee is counted.
Your contract
The end date, the notice period and any fee for leaving early.
Your equipment
Whether the reader takes contactless, and whether it can move to a new account.
Your product list
Whether it exports, so nobody types it in again.
