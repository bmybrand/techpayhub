Questions owners ask first

Can I keep the card reader I have?

Sometimes. It depends on the model and on the processor contract behind it. Put the make and model in your quote request and the quote will say whether it can stay.

What should I send with my request?

The number of counters, what you use today and, if you have it nearby, a recent card-processing statement. Leave out card numbers and passwords.

Do you set up more than one location?

Yes. Shops with several locations are one of the six trades we set up, with stock, prices and reports covering all of them.

What happens when the internet drops?

We check the signal at your counter before we quote and plan for it. How much you can do offline depends on the equipment you choose.

Who trains my staff?

We do, on the day we install. Your team rings up real sales while we are still in the room.

Will I see every cost before I agree?

The quote lists the equipment, the monthly costs and what is left out, so you can compare it line by line.
